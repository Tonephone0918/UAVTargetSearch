# Progressive Stage Mechanism Caption

English:

> Stage-level mechanism statistics for progressive conservativeness curricula. The early stages stay at the hard-safe / safe layer, while the threshold-only curriculum switches to the recursive feasible layer in the mid and late stages. The safeearly variant switches to an H=2 stronger layer in the late stage, which changes gate and dead-end statistics but should not be interpreted as evidence of a uniformly better final learned policy.

中文：

> Progressive conservativeness curriculum 的阶段机制统计。early 阶段主要停留在 hard-safe / safe 层；threshold-only curriculum 在 mid 和 late 阶段切入 recursive feasible layer。safeearly 变体在 late 阶段切入 H=2 stronger layer，这会改变 gate 和 dead-end 统计，但不应被解释为最终 learned policy 稳定更优。
